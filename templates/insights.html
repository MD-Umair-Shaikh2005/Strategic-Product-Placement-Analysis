💡 Insights & Recommendations

Data-driven findings and actionable strategies

🔍 Key Findings
1️⃣ Optimal Placement Strategy

Premium shelf positions and high-traffic areas consistently outperform other locations. End-cap displays show 2-3x higher conversion rates.

2️⃣ Category Performance

Fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) categories demonstrate highest profit margins. Seasonal products show predictable demand patterns.

3️⃣ Customer Behavior

Customers show strong preference for products at eye-level (45-70 inches). Bundle recommendations increase average transaction value by 35%.

4️⃣ Revenue Optimization

Strategic placement changes could boost revenue by 20-25% with minimal cost increase. Cross-category placement yields highest customer engagement.
